alt_route How to play

Tap a clear arrow. Keep your hearts.

An arrow leaves when the lane in front of its head is empty. A blocked tap costs one heart. Clear the board to win the level.

Clear one arrow

The arrow crawls off along its own body. Arrows nearby can change their shape once that space opens.

Hearts, then an empty board

A tap on an arrow that cannot leave breaks one heart. Keep clearing until every arrow has left the picture. Easy, Medium, and Hard give you 2, 6, and 10 minutes, and finishing in time adds 50% extra coins.

VS Boss

Open it from home or from a collection. You take turns tapping a clear arrow. A longer arrow scores more points. Freeze, Fire, Eagle Eye, and Blinder are the powers for the match.

Accounts

Google can restore it. Guest stays on the phone.

Google keeps your progress and can restore it. Guest progress stays on the phone. In an online match, other players see your display name and avatar.

Google

Google keeps the save

Sign in with Google and the game can restore that progress. The save includes your display name, avatar, coins, power-ups, chests, daily streak, spin dates, and cleared levels.

auto_fix_high Powers

Powers and the store

On a level you can use 1 Arrow, 3 Arrow, Eraser, and Eye. In VS Boss, same-phone play, and online 1v1 you can use Freeze, Fire, Eagle Eye, and Blinder. The store spends coins. An ad can add coins, hearts, or a doubled reward.

Level powers

1 Arrow

Clears one arrow that can currently escape.

3 Arrow

Clears up to three arrows that can currently escape.

Eraser

Lets you choose an arrow and take it off the board.

Eye

Briefly highlights arrows whose lanes are clear right now.

Duel powers

Used in VS Boss, same-phone play, and online 1v1.

Freeze

Tap one arrow to freeze it, then play another.

Fire

Burn any arrow for its points, then continue the turn.

Eagle Eye

Points you at a playable arrow and the points it is worth.

Blinder

The opponent’s powers go dark on their next turn.

FAQ

Straight answers

Can I play offline? add

Collection levels and a Guest save stay on the phone. Online 1v1 and a Google restore use a connection.

What do ads give? add

Ads can give extra coins, hearts, or a doubled reward. The store spends coins.

What is the time bonus? add

Finish in time for 50% extra coins. Easy is 2 minutes, Medium is 6, and Hard is 10.

What does Google restore? add

A Google sign-in can restore the save: display name, avatar, coins, power-ups, chests, daily streak, spin dates, and cleared levels. Guest progress stays on that phone and is not restored as a Google save.

Is this game for children under 13? add

No. Winding Arrows is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13.
